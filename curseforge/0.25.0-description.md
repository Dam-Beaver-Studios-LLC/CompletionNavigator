# Completion Navigator

Most completion addons answer **"what am I missing?"** and hand you a list of several thousand things. Completion Navigator answers a different question: **"what should I do next, and what should I do while I am there?"**

It reads what you have already earned across your Warband, works out what is actually reachable now, ranks it, groups the nearby pieces together, and routes you through them.

---

## Plan a zone once, walk it once

A quest is not one place. It is a **pick up**, a **do**, and a **turn in** — and treating it as a single dot on a map is exactly why you end up crossing a zone and coming back.

Completion Navigator knows the difference:

- Objectives within about seventy yards collapse into a single **stop**.
- The route is solved stop to stop, then improved with a second pass to cut out doubling back.
- Within a stop, things are ordered the way you would actually do them: collect the quests, do the work, hand them back.
- Work that batches with other work **scores higher**, so the recommendation agrees with the route instead of sending you across the zone for one quest.

`/cn zone` prints the sweep. *"3 things here — pick up 2, turn in 1."*

## See the plan on your map

**New in 0.25.0.** The route is drawn on the world map as numbered pins, one per stop, in the order you would walk them.

- Hover a pin to see what you do when you arrive, in order.
- Click it to navigate there.
- The next stop wears the addon's blue; later stops are dimmed, so *where now* is answerable at a glance.
- One pin per stop, not per objective — a single pin reading "3 — pick up 2, do 4, hand in 1" tells you more than twelve overlapping pins on one camp.

Toggle with `/cn pins`, or from the options panel. Additive and read-only: they appear only on a map you have opened.

## Quests you have not picked up yet

The exclamation marks standing in front of you are often the cheapest next action available. Completion Navigator reads available quests from the map, not just your quest log, so *"go and collect that one"* is an answer it can give — weighted above an accepted quest you have not started, because the walk is short and it unlocks whatever follows.

## Navigation without another addon

A native on-screen arrow, in the addon's own colours, that tells you whether you are walking toward your target or away from it. TomTom is used if you have it and is not required. HandyNotes, AllTheThings and BtWQuests are read when present, and nothing breaks when they are absent.

## Warband-aware

Account-wide unlocks are recognised as account-wide. Something another character already earned does not get recommended to this one, and the reason line says which character did it.

## Show only what you care about

Hide any objective type you are not working on — quests, pets, mounts, toys, appearances, reputations, professions, currencies, exploration, rares. Hidden types drop out of the recommendations **and** out of the route, so you are not walked to something you explicitly said you did not want. Collection totals still count everything.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/cn` | What to do next |
| `/cn zone` | The full sweep for this zone, stop by stop |
| `/cn pins` | Route pins on the world map — `on`, `off`, `refresh` |
| `/cn go` | Navigate to the top recommendation |
| `/cn why <id>` | Why this is being recommended, and what is blocking it |
| `/cn show` | Choose which kinds of objective appear |
| `/cn goal <id>` | Pin a goal and weight everything toward it |
| `/cn vault` | Great Vault progress |
| `/cn breakdown` | Collection totals by category |
| `/cn setup` | Full rescan |
| `/cn help` | Everything |

There is a window (`/cn ui`), a minimap button, tooltip lines on items and NPCs, and an optional LibDataBroker feed.

## Notes

- Where the game does not provide a trustworthy denominator, this addon reports counts rather than inventing a completion percentage.
- Nothing is taken over without being asked. Auto-advancing the waypoint and rare alerts are off by default.
- No external server, no account required, no data leaves your machine.
